Common Questions

Everything you need to know before getting in touch. If your question isn't answered here, message us directly.

Which areas of law do you cover?
Corporate, Criminal, Family, Property, and Immigration Law — for individuals and businesses throughout the United Kingdom.
How do I book a consultation?
Message us on Telegram or send an email using the contact details below. Briefly describe your matter and we will respond promptly with clear next steps.
What should I include in my first message?
Your name, the type of legal matter, a short summary of the situation, and any urgent deadlines you are aware of. This helps us respond accurately and quickly.
Is my information kept confidential?
Absolutely. We treat the details of every matter with strict professionalism and discretion. Your information is never shared without your explicit consent.
How quickly will you respond?
We aim to respond to all enquiries within 24 hours. For urgent legal matters, please indicate the urgency in your message and we will prioritise accordingly.
Do you offer a free initial consultation?
We offer an initial discussion to assess your matter and advise on the appropriate next steps. Get in touch to find out how we can assist with your specific situation.
